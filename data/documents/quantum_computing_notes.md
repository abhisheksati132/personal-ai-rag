# Quantum Computing and Entanglement

Quantum computing utilizes qubits that exist in superpositions of state |0> and |1>.
The key quantum algorithm for factoring integers is Shor's Algorithm, which achieves exponential speedup over classical algorithms in O((log N)^3) time.
Grover's Algorithm provides a quadratic speedup for unstructured database search in O(sqrt(N)) time.
Quantum teleportation protocol requires one Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen (EPR) entangled pair and two classical bits transmitted over a classical channel.
